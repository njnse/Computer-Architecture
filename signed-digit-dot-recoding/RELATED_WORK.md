# Primary literature and verified scope

- Andrew D. Booth, *A Signed Binary Multiplication Technique*, [DOI 10.1093/qjmam/4.2.236](https://doi.org/10.1093/qjmam/4.2.236). The primary publisher abstract/title/author were verified. This is the foundational signed recoding reference, not a claim that the original article specifies this artifact's radix-4 group scheduler. Our exact radix-4 overlapping-bit identity is stated in `reference.py`, independently checked against direct multiplication, and executed in the RTL baseline.
- Jorge Albericio, Patrick Judd, Alberto Delmas, Sayeh Sharify, Andreas Moshovos, *Bit-Pragmatic Deep Neural Network Computing*, [primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1610.06920v1). Essential activation bits and bit-serial activation/bit-parallel weight arithmetic motivate the shared-plane baseline. Our single-group engine does not reproduce Pragmatic's multi-lane synchronization, tiles, or memory system. It explicitly handles signed activations, including minimum signed values.
- Alberto Delmas, Patrick Judd, Sayeh Sharify, Andreas Moshovos, *Dynamic Stripes: Exploiting the Dynamic Precision Requirements of Activation Values in Neural Networks*, [primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1706.00504v1). Group OR, leading/trailing offsets and contiguous counter scheduling are the relevant rules. We compare a separate counter RTL for both TC and SM, and a separate radix-4 counter, not just fixed W-plane arithmetic. These are mechanism controls, not complete accelerator reproductions.
- Jiajun Wu, Jiajun Zhou, Yizhao Gao, Yuhao Ding, Ngai Wong, Hayden Kwok-Hay So, *MSD: Mixing Signed Digit Representations for Hardware-efficient DNN Acceleration on FPGA with Heterogeneous Resources*, [primary author-hosted full text](https://hub.hku.hk/bitstream/10722/369963/1/wu_fccm2023.pdf). The primary text describes restricted signed-digit weight quantization, limiting effectual bits and balancing LUT bit-serial and DSP bit-parallel resources. It makes clear that representation and heterogeneous resource co-design are established research directions. We do not implement its restricted quantizer, tuning, mixed engines, or end-to-end accelerator; our comparison is exact activation-side recoding with complete integer range.

Known sign-magnitude and signed-digit arithmetic are not novel here. This is a bounded experimental comparison of execution, conversion/storage cost, signed parameter correctness and newly trained workload evidence. The literature review is selective; it does not establish a publication-level novelty gap. No prior paper's system speedup is reused as our measurement.

RTL and the handshake scoreboard are adapted from [weight-qualified-bitplane-dot at the pinned commit](https://github.com/njnse/Computer-Architecture/tree/394ce36c7677837dc95f7e8621eb4ba0313bb2bf/weight-qualified-bitplane-dot). The new magnitude conversion, widened coefficients, independent radix-4 engine, genuine signed hidden traces, and new numerical/protocol validations distinguish this artifact from the predecessor. All retained measurements are newly executed.
